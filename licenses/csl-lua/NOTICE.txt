Packaging and modifications
---------------------------

Exact file provenance, original hashes, distributed hashes, and license names
are recorded in vendor/csl-lua/files.json. Standalone module names are recorded
in vendor/csl-lua/modules.json. Local engine fixes and the partial standalone
packaging are documented in vendor/csl-lua/MODIFICATIONS.md. Original versions
of modified engine files are retained in vendor/csl-lua/original/.

The processor source and data are supplied with the source distribution.
The native Lua/LPeg/luautf8 runtime is recorded separately with its sources,
build configuration, licenses, and provenance under vendor/csl-lua/runtime/.
No attribution text is inserted into documents or exported bibliographies.
