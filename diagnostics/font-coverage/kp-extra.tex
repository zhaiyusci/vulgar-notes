\section*{KpMath: additional sans Greek glyphs}
The standard unicode-math commands do not automatically select these private-use glyphs.
\begin{description}
\item[Default Greek] $\alpha\beta\Gamma\Delta$
\item[Standard sans command] $\symsfup{\alpha\beta\Gamma\Delta}$
\item[KpMath upright sans] $\msansalpha\msansbeta\msansGamma\msansDelta$
\item[KpMath italic sans] $\mitsansalpha\mitsansbeta\mitsansGamma\mitsansDelta$
\end{description}
